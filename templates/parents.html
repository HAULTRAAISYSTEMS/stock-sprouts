Parents & Educators

What Stock Quest is — and what it is not.

{{ icon('check', 'ic gold') }} What kids learn

Earning, spending, and saving · needs vs. wants · what a stock is · why prices move up and down · dividends · diversification · corrections and crashes — experienced safely in a simulator before real money ever enters the picture.

{{ icon('lock', 'ic gold') }} Safety guardrails

All money is pretend money, labeled everywhere. No real brokerage accounts, no real trading, no chat or contact with strangers, no gambling mechanics, no ads. Parent-owned accounts; the Money Bank teaches saving, not spending.

{{ icon('grad', 'ic gold') }} Built for ages 6–18

Four age brackets (6–8, 9–12, 13–15, 16–18) adjust the language, the lessons, and the rewards. Younger kids collect and spot; teens work with real market prices, headlines, and simulated storms.

{{ icon('chart', 'ic gold') }} Proof of learning

Every quest ends with a checklist of what was learned, XP, and badges you can review together. Ask your kid to explain the Correction Gauntlet at dinner — if they can teach it, they learned it.
