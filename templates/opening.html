Parents & Educators

For parents & educators

Stock Quest teaches real money ideas — earning, saving, needs vs. wants, owning slices of companies, and surviving market storms — through short quests and games. Every dollar in the game is pretend money: no real trading, no real accounts, no chat with strangers, no ads.

Kids 6–18 play in age-matched brackets, earn Stock Coins and XP, and unlock new districts as they learn. Read the full safety note.
