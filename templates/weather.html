Why does weather happen?

Sunny

Prices go up

Good news — a hit product, big sales, happy customers — makes more people want to buy the stock. More buyers than sellers pushes the price up.

Stormy

Prices go down

Bad news or scary headlines make people sell. More sellers than buyers pushes the price down. Storms feel bad but they always pass.

Cloudy

Prices stay flat

Sometimes nobody has strong news and prices barely move. The market is just waiting — like clouds before the sky decides.

The big lesson: you cannot control the weather — but you can dress for it. Patient owners stay invested through storms instead of panic-selling at the bottom.
