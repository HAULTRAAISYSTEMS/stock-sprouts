How it works

1

Spot brands you love

See a swoosh, a golden arch, a game you play every day? Tap "Spot it!" and unlock that company's card.

2

Buy pretend slices

Every player starts with $1,000 in pretend cash. Buy slices of the companies you spotted — a stock is a slice of a real company.

3

Grow your money tree

When your companies do well, your tree grows and drops coins. Weather the stormy days — storms always pass.

A note for parents: Stock Sprouts runs on real market prices with pretend money only. There is no real trading, no brokerage account, and no way to spend real money in the game. It teaches ownership, patience, and how markets really move — the basics, the highs, the lows, and the corrections. Family leagues and subscriptions are coming soon.
